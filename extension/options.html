      Settings

      Keyboard shortcut

      Opens the snippet panel in the field you are typing in, like the double tap.

      Typing a snippet's abbreviation in a text field replaces it with the snippet, and Ctrl+Z brings the typed text back. When abbreviations wait for Space, Tab, or Enter, a space stays after the snippet, while Tab and Enter only expand it. This does not work in Google Docs.

      Sites where the double tap and abbreviations are off

        No sites yet. The double tap and abbreviations work on every site.

        A site includes its subdomains: example.com also covers mail.example.com. On these sites the keyboard shortcut and the right-click menu still open the panel. The button next to Close at the top of the panel turns the current site off or back on.

        Click in a text box, a rich-text editor, or a Google Docs document and tap the key twice quickly. In the panel, pick a snippet with the mouse, the arrow keys and Enter, or Ctrl+2 to Ctrl+9. Ctrl+1 creates a snippet, and if text is selected when the panel opens, the new snippet starts with that text. Snippets have no length limit.

        Backup

        Adds the snippets in a backup file. Snippets you already have are skipped.
